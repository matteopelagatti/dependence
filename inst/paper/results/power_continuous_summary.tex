\begin{tabnote}
Critical values are size-adjusted from $5000$ null datasets per cell, obtained by permuting $Y$, except for the statistics depending on the data only through the ranks, which are distribution-free under independence and are calibrated once per $(n,p,q)$ from $20000$ null datasets. Power is over $10000$ independent draws from the alternative, so the largest standard error is $0{\cdot}005$. Orders follow rule (12), giving $p=q=2, 3, 4$; the binary expansion depth for BET and BEAST is $d=2, 3, 3$, matched to the number of directions examined. The mean rank is the average rank, one being best, within each combination.
The data-driven rank tests of Kallenberg and Ledwina (1999) use orthonormal Legendre polynomials of the ranks with the modified Schwarz selection rule, with $d(n)=4$ diagonal components for $T_{S2}$ and $d(n)=2$ for $V$.
